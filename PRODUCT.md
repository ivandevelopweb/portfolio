## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners, service providers, and product teams looking for a developer who can build a website or a broader digital product. They visit to judge the quality and range of Ivan's work, understand the services, and decide how to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Ivan's portfolio presents his web development work and makes it easy for prospective clients to contact him about a project.

## Positioning

An independent web developer who can take a business-focused web project from its structure and interface through implementation, integrations, and launch. The scope includes business websites, e-commerce, web applications, APIs, and automation.

## Operating Context

Visitors can browse five live project examples, switch between English, Ukrainian, and Russian at `/`, `/ua`, and `/rus`, and reach Ivan through Telegram, email, or the Netlify contact form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The existing application is built with React and Vite and is deployed through Netlify.
- The contact form posts to `/.netlify/functions/send-contact` and collects a name, Telegram handle, and message. Its company field is a spam honeypot.
- Preserve working locale navigation, contact behavior, social links, project destinations, and responsive access.
- The portfolio may describe broad web development, e-commerce, web applications, API integrations, Telegram products, and automation, but should not imply every capability is demonstrated by a listed project.
- Do not invent performance figures, client outcomes, client logos, reviews, awards, or years of experience.

## Brand Commitments

- Retain a dark, technical character and the subdued perspective grid as a recognizable motif.
- Keep the existing portrait and portfolio screenshots unless an asset is demonstrably unsuitable.
